How it works

Install once. Keep working.

There's no new tool to learn. Focus sits beside the agents and apps you already use.

  1. Install the Focus CLI

    Sign in with your work email, then connect Slack and email. Focus registers your agent tools on that machine.

  2. Work as usual

    Keep using Claude Code, Cursor or Codex. Every coding chat becomes an agent with a permanent record of what it did.

  3. Watch your agents improve

    Skills you and your teammates share land on every machine, for every agent tool. Your inbox gets shorter as Focus takes the overhead.

Your AI, not ours

Focus's brain runs on your own Claude, Cursor or Codex subscription, on your own machine.

Your data stays with you

Agents query company databases read-only through an Outpost inside your network. Every query is signed and audited.

You stay in control

Drafts wait for your OK. Shared skills need the owner's approval before a colleague gets them.
