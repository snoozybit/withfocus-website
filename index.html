Install once. Keep working.

There's no new tool to learn. Focus sits beside the agents and apps you already use.

  1. Install the Focus CLI

    Sign in with your work email, then connect Slack and Gmail. Focus registers your agent tools on that machine.

  2. Work as usual

    Keep using Claude Code, Cursor or Codex. Every coding chat becomes an agent with a permanent record of what it did.

  3. Watch your agents improve

    Skills you and your teammates approve land on every machine, for every agent tool. Your inbox gets shorter as Focus takes the overhead.

Questions

Which agents and apps does it work with?

Claude Code, Cursor and Codex for coding, and Slack and Gmail for messages. GitHub and Notion comments are next.

Does my code or data leave my machine?

Your code stays on your machine. What passes through Focus is the skills your team approves and the messages it routes, so your teammates' agents can get them. Focus's brain runs on your own Claude, Cursor or Codex subscription, and agents reach company databases read-only through Outpost, inside your network, with every query signed and audited.

What size of team is it for?

From one developer up. It's most useful on a team: a skill one person's agent learns reaches everyone's agents.

What do I need to install?

The Focus CLI on each machine. You sign in with your work email and connect Slack and Gmail. Your agent tools are found automatically.

How do skills get approved?

Each skill has an owner. A skill reaches a colleague's agents only after the owner approves it, and you can see where every skill came from.
